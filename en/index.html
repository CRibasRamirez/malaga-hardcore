About us

Málaga Hardcore is a non-profit cultural association born from Málaga's underground scene. From day one we have worked to give local bands a stage, bring bands from abroad and create a meeting point for everyone who feels this music is more than a style: a way of life.

We organise concerts, gatherings and DIY events, always standing for respect, inclusion and mutual support within the community. Every event is a collective effort made by and for the people of the scene.

If you share these values and want to help out, play or simply be part of it, this is your home.

How it all started

It all began when we came together to pay tribute to Málaga's underground bands. That tribute became this mural, painted by @desska.98, with the names of the scene.

Seeing all those names together, we realised it was the line-up of a killer festival. That's how Málaga Hardcore Fest and the association were born.
